# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We welcome technical disagreement, independent reproduction, corrections, and
careful criticism. Participants must be respectful, specific, and focused on
the work. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct,
deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private data are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Use GitHub's private report-to-maintainers function for conduct reports. Do not
open a public issue containing private evidence. Maintainers may edit, hide,
lock, or remove material and may temporarily or permanently restrict accounts
when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, prior behavior, and
whether repair is possible. The automated moderator may route and label content,
but only a human maintainer makes conduct decisions.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially
representing the project elsewhere.
